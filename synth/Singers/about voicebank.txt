Indirectly supported languages:
- Tagalog
- Vietnamese

Supported expressions:
- tension (curve)
- velocity (curve)
- gender (curve)
- dynamics (curve)
- pitch expressiveness (curve)

Notes:
- If your video card supports RTX, we recommend set Preferences - Rendering - Machine Learning Runner as "DirectML" and select your GPU below. This will significantly increase the rendering speed.
- To add expressions that the singer has, select Project -> Expressions -> Add all expressions suggested by renders.
- To render singer's pitch you need to right click on note and press Ctrl+R.
- To use languages dictionary you need to select the appropriate phonemizer (underneath where the singers are selected on the track).
- To make your vocals more expressive, you can use multiple vocal modes or raise and lower the "tension".

About Render Steps:
- Best settings: Acoustic 50, Variance 40, Pitch 10
If rendering is slow, try lowering the number of Acoustic render steps, but this will reduce the sound quality

All additional information can be found on our website: https://lunaiproject.github.io/
