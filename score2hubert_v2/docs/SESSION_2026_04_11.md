## Key Discoveries

### 1. Global HuBERT Variance is Misleading

Ran `scripts/diag_conditional_variance.py` across ACE, GTSinger ZH/JA/EN.

**Global stats showed everything green** (std_pattern_cos > 0.997, std_ratio < 1.06).
But this hides within-phoneme issues because between-phoneme variance dominates (95%+).

### 2. Per-Phoneme-Per-Pitch Conditional Variance: Actually Fine

Within each (phoneme, pitch) bucket, all datasets have similar spread:
- ACE within-std: 0.233
- GTSinger ZH: 0.227
- GTSinger JA: 0.228
- GTSinger EN: 0.232

**Ratio vs ACE: ZH=0.975, JA=0.977, EN=1.012.** None worse than ACE.

### 3. Cross-Dataset Centroid Misalignment: THE Key Finding

`scripts/diag_centroid_alignment.py` — same-phoneme centroid cos between ACE and GTSinger:

| | All buckets | Vowels | /i/ | /k/ | /s/ |
|---|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| GTSinger ZH | **0.810** | 0.933 | 0.960 | 0.772 | 0.780 |
| GTSinger JA | **0.805** | 0.913 | 0.932 | 0.801 | 0.791 |
| GTSinger EN | **0.755** | 0.874 | 0.920 | 0.656 | 0.751 |

**Even same-language (ZH vs ZH) vowel centroids only 0.933.**
Consonants much worse (0.66-0.80) — alignment boundary sensitivity.

User's hypothesis: label inconsistency (different G2P/aligners). Partially confirmed.

### 4. Pitch Error Diagnostic

|---|:-:|:-:|:-:|

ACE has worst tail (31% >100c) but tightest median.
UTAU is cleanest overall.

### 5. UTAU Bank Quality

8/10 banks excellent (median <25c). Two excluded:
- **関西しのび**: 130c median, -149c signed mean — >1 semitone flat
- **中部つるぎ**: 37c median but -64c signed mean — systematic flat bias

## Decisions Made

1. **GTSinger ZH excluded** — centroid misalignment + not needed (ACE covers zh)
2. **GTSinger EN+JA Control_Group only** — minimize technique variance
3. **Skipped MFA** — GTSinger has professional annotations; centroid differences partly explained by F0 bucketing contamination + speaker variation. Will revisit if post-training shows phoneme confusion.
4. **Started ACE-only training** — fastest path to working model. v1 Plan C validated this approach.
5. **Training config**: batch 48, 50K steps, lr 1.5e-4, cosine schedule, FP16
